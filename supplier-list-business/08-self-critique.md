# Self-Critique

I reviewed the kit as if I were a sceptical buyer, a payment platform, and a competitor. Weak points and what was done about each:

| # | Weakness | Severity | What I changed / what you must do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The starter leads are weak evidence.** Most come from "Top 10" articles that the factories wrote about themselves, and MOQs/prices are self-reported | High | Every lead is marked UNVERIFIED. Added SOP Step 0 telling you to collect 30+ leads directly on Alibaba/Made-in-China/1688 with filters. Expect half to fail |
| 2 | **The information is mostly free.** Anyone can search Alibaba | High | The product's value is *verification + samples + consent + updates*. The sales page, product template and marketing all show proof (sample photos, weights, scores, dates). If you skip vetting, the product isn't worth buying |
| 3 | **Sample budget was too low** in the first draft (€150–400) | Medium | Raised to €250–570 with a per-sample breakdown; break-even updated |
| 4 | **Conflict of interest:** referral commissions could bias rankings | Medium | SOP Step 5b: score *before* asking about commission. Disclosure in product, sales page and FAQ |
| 5 | **Contacts go stale:** sales reps leave, WhatsApp numbers die | Medium | Always list the store link as well; quarterly re-check message (#6); "Last verified" date on every card |
| 6 | **Refund/chargeback risk** from buyers who feel cheated | Medium | Conditional response guarantee (30% non-response → refund) + EU digital-content consent checkbox. Keeps refunds low and fair |
| 7 | **Privacy (GDPR)** when publishing named people's numbers | Medium | Consent message (#3), business contacts only, removal process, privacy notice |
| 8 | **The list will leak.** Someone will share the PDF | Low–Medium | Sell access to a live Notion page (updates are the value; a leaked PDF goes stale). Accept some leakage; it's also free advertising |
| 9 | **Conflicting data** (e.g. Hongyu: Guangzhou vs Dongguan, MOQ 30 vs 100) | Low | Flagged in the CSV notes; resolved during vetting |
| 10 | **Scoring bug:** blank cells counted as full points in Excel | Fixed | Formulas use `N()`. Tested with LibreOffice: full row = 95/TOP PICK, partial = IN PROGRESS, red flag = REJECTED |
| 11 | **Platform facts change** (fees, VAT handling, whether a platform still exists) | Low | Plan tells you to check current pricing/allowed-products pages before signing up |
| 12 | **Saturated English market** | Medium | Spanish-language angle (Spain + LatAm) suggested in the plan, marketing and sales page FAQ |
| 13 | **Putian/footwear expansion** carries high counterfeit risk | Medium | Warning in SOP Step 0; strict red-flag checks before listing any footwear factory |

## What this kit can't do for you

- **Verify suppliers.** That needs you to message them, receive samples and look up registrations. I can't receive samples or log into Alibaba/1688 for you.
- **Guarantee sales.** Demand exists, but income depends on your content and how trustworthy you look.
- **Replace a local tax/legal check** once you're earning.

## Honest verdict

This is a viable small business **if** you do the vetting work and show it publicly. Without real samples and verification, it's indistinguishable from the scam lists it competes with. Realistic first goal: 20–50 sales in the first 1–2 months, then grow through referral commissions and a sourcing service. Those are worth more per customer than the list itself.
