# Vetting Procedure (SOP)

## Step 0. Get more leads (the 15 starter leads aren't enough)

Most starter leads came from "Top 10 factories" articles that **the factories wrote about themselves**. Expect some of them to fail. Aim for **30+ leads to end with 10–15 listed**. Add leads by searching directly:

- **Alibaba.com:** search "heavyweight hoodie manufacturer", "blank hoodie OEM", "custom streetwear private label". Filters: *Verified Supplier*, *Manufacturer*, *Trade Assurance*, *5+ years*, location *Guangdong*
- **Made-in-China.com:** same searches. Filter *Audited Supplier*
- **1688.com** (via browser translate, buy through an agent): search 重磅卫衣 工厂 (heavyweight hoodie factory), 卫衣 定制 源头工厂 (custom hoodie source factory), 小单快反 (small-order quick turnaround). 1688 factories are often the cheapest, but rarely speak English, so list them in a separate "via agent" section
- **Clusters to target:** Humen (Dongguan) for streetwear/knitwear, Guangzhou for fast fashion, Ningbo/Shaoxing for basics, Jinjiang/Putian for footwear (be careful: Putian is also the centre of fake sneakers, so apply red-flag checks strictly)

**Why not Yupoo or Taobao?** Yupoo is a photo host with no seller verification, and most clothing stores on it sell replicas. Taobao is retail, mostly resellers. Neither works as a source for a *verified* list.

Run **every** supplier through this before it goes in a paid list. Log the results in `02-supplier-tracker.xlsx`. The tracker turns your answers into a 0–100 score.

Time per supplier: about 30–60 minutes plus waiting for replies.

## Step 1. Instant red flags (stop if any are true)

- [ ] Sells branded goods it doesn't own: logos of Nike, Supreme, Chrome Hearts etc., "1:1", "top quality copy", "UA", "mirror"
- [ ] Asks you to pay outside the platform (bank transfer to a personal account, PayPal friends & family, crypto) before any trust exists
- [ ] Company name on invoice/bank account ≠ name on the store
- [ ] Price far below every other supplier for the same spec
- [ ] Refuses any video call or live video of the factory floor
- [ ] Store less than 1 year old **and** no registration record found

If any box is ticked, mark the supplier **REJECTED** and move on.

## Step 2. Company registration check (free)

1. Ask the supplier for their **Chinese legal company name** and **Unified Social Credit Code** (统一社会信用代码, 18 characters). Real companies give this without hesitating. Message #2 in the outreach file asks for it.
2. Search it on **gsxt.gov.cn** (National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System). The site is Chinese only, so use browser translation. The free aggregators **Qichacha (qcc.com)** and **Tianyancha (tianyancha.com)** are easier to use.
3. Check and record:
   - **Status** is 存续 / 在业 (active). Anything else is a red flag
   - **Registered date**: older is better
   - **Business scope** (经营范围) includes 生产 / 制造 (production/manufacturing), not only 销售 / 贸易 (sales/trade). Trade-only means a trading company, so note it (not automatically bad, but they're not the factory)
   - **Registered capital**
   - **Abnormal operation list** (经营异常) or court enforcement records: red flag
   - The **address** matches what the store shows

## Step 3. Platform signals (Alibaba / Made-in-China / 1688)

| Signal | Good | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Years on platform | 3+ | |
| Verified Supplier / Audited Supplier | Yes | Means an outside company checked the business legally exists. **Does not** check quality |
| Gold Supplier | Ignore | It's a paid membership, nothing more |
| Trade Assurance | Yes | Escrow protection when paying on Alibaba |
| Response rate | 90%+ | |
| Reviews / transaction history | Present, consistent | Read the 1–3 star reviews |
| Business type | "Manufacturer" + factory photos | |

## Step 4. Talk to them

Send message #1 (inquiry). Score:
- **Response time** (under 24h = good)
- **English quality / clarity**: can a beginner buyer work with them?
- **Answers specific questions**: GSM, fabric composition, MOQ per colour, sample cost, lead time
- **Video check**: ask for a 1–2 min live video of the sewing floor holding a paper with today's date

## Step 5. Samples (top 3–5 only)

Order one sample hoodie or tee from each. On arrival:
- **Weigh it.** Hoodie GSM claims are often inflated. A 500gsm XL hoodie usually weighs about 900g–1.1kg; under ~750g suggests the fabric is lighter than claimed
- Check stitching, cuffs, shrinkage after 1 wash, print/embroidery quality, and how the label is sewn
- Photograph everything in daylight. These photos go in the product and your marketing
- Compare **sample time quoted vs. actual**

## Step 5b. Score BEFORE you ask about commissions

Fill in the score in the tracker **before** sending message #4 (referral/commission). That way commission deals can't influence who becomes a Top Pick. It's also what the disclosure in your product promises.

## Step 6. Consent + details

Send message #3 (permission to list). Only list them if they say **yes**. Record:
- Who agreed (name + date), and a screenshot of the reply
- The **business** contact they want public (company email / WhatsApp business / sales rep's work account)
- **Always add the store link too** (Alibaba / Made-in-China). Sales reps change jobs and their WhatsApp stops answering, but the store stays. Buyers also keep Trade Assurance protection by ordering through the store

## Step 7. Score and decide

The tracker calculates the score from these weights:

| Category | Max points |
|---|---|
| Registration active + manufacturing scope | 20 |
| Years in business / on platform | 10 |
| Verified/Audited status + Trade Assurance | 10 |
| Responsiveness (time + clarity) | 15 |
| Factory video provided | 10 |
| Sample quality | 25 |
| Sample price & speed vs. quote | 5 |
| Consent to be listed | 5 (and required) |
| **Total** | **100** |

- **80–100:** "Top Pick"
- **65–79:** listed
- **Under 65, or no consent:** not listed

## Step 8. Re-check every 3 months

Send a short "still taking small orders? MOQ/prices still the same?" message (#6). Update or remove the supplier. Put the "Last verified" date on every profile card.
