# Business Plan

## 1. The niche

**Starting niche: factories for small streetwear brands.** That means heavyweight hoodies, tees and sweatpants with custom logos (private label), plus branding suppliers for labels, hang tags and mailers.

Why this niche:
- **Lots of demand.** Thousands of people start clothing brands every month, and most get stuck finding a factory.
- **Buyers will pay.** Someone about to spend €1,000–5,000 on a first production run happily pays €20–50 to avoid a bad factory.
- **Low minimums exist.** Several factories advertise 30–100 pieces per design, so beginners can actually use the list.
- **It's legal and platform-safe**, unlike rep lists, which get payment accounts frozen.

**Angle to stand out: Spanish-speaking buyers.** Almost every supplier list is in English. A list with a Spanish version (Spain + Latin America) faces far less competition. Sell both languages from day one if you can.

Later niches, once the first one sells: sneaker/footwear OEM, bags and accessories, jewelry, phone accessories, packaging, gym wear.

## 2. The offer

| Tier | What's included | Price (suggested) |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter List** | 10–15 verified suppliers with profile cards, MOQs, price ranges, sample costs, contact person | €19–29 |
| **Brand Launch Pack** | Starter List + branding suppliers (labels, tags, mailers) + message templates + vetting checklist | €39–59 |
| **Done-with-you** | Pack + a 30-min call + you introduce them to 2 suppliers personally | €99–149 |
| **Updates** (optional) | Quarterly refresh + 3–5 new suppliers per quarter | €5/month or €12/quarter |

Start with one tier at a low launch price (e.g. €15 for the first 20 buyers) to get reviews fast. Raise prices once you have 5–10 testimonials.

## 3. Extra income (most sellers miss this)

- **Referral commission from factories.** Many factories pay 3–10% on orders from buyers you send, or give you a free sample budget. Ask with message #4 in `04-outreach-messages.md`. **Tell your buyers this happens**, both because it's honest and because hiding it destroys trust if found out.
- **Sourcing service.** Once you know the factories, charge 5–10% to manage orders for clients who don't want to do it themselves.
- **Agent affiliate links** (Superbuy, CSSBuy, Sugargoo, etc.) for buyers purchasing small quantities on 1688/Taobao.

## 4. Startup costs

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Samples from 3–5 factories (≈ €30–60 sample + €30–50 express shipping each, plus possible import VAT) | €250–500 |
| Company lookup reports (optional, Qichacha/Tianyancha) | €0–50 |
| Domain + simple site (or Gumroad/Payhip page free) | €0–20 |
| Canva/Notion for the PDF | €0 |
| **Total** | **≈ €250–570** |

Tip: ask factories to deduct the sample cost from a future bulk order, or to ship several samples together. Some will, which cuts the cost.

Samples are the biggest cost but also your best marketing: unboxing and quality-comparison videos sell the list.

## 5. Break-even

At €29 per sale minus platform fees (~10%), you get about €26 per sale. €500 of costs needs **about 20 sales** to break even (about 37 sales at a €15 launch price). Everything after that is profit, apart from your time spent on updates.

## 6. Where to sell

| Platform | Fees | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Payhip** | 5% (free plan) + payment processor | Handles EU VAT for digital goods. Good if you're in the EU |
| **Lemon Squeezy** | ~5% + 50¢ | Merchant of record: handles VAT/sales tax globally |
| **Gumroad** | ~10% + processing | Easy, but reviews complain about payout holds and support. Avoid "guaranteed" claims to reduce chargebacks |
| **Your own Shopify/Stripe** | Monthly fee + ~2–3% | More control, but you handle VAT yourself |

**Recommendation:** use Payhip or Lemon Squeezy. They handle VAT on digital sales to EU consumers for you. Fees and features change, so check each platform's current pricing page and allowed-products rules before signing up.

## 7. 30-day launch plan

| Days | Task |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Set up a business email and WhatsApp/WeChat. Create Alibaba buyer account. Load leads into the tracker |
| 3–10 | Run vetting SOP on all 15 leads. Send inquiry messages (#1). Drop anything that fails red-flag checks |
| 7–14 | Get quotes. Send consent-to-list (#3) and referral (#4) messages. Order samples from the top 3–5 |
| 10–20 | Start posting content (see marketing plan), e.g. "I'm testing 5 hoodie factories". Build an audience *before* launch |
| 14–24 | Samples arrive: photograph, weigh (GSM check), and review. Fill in profile cards |
| 20–25 | Build the PDF/Notion product. Set up Payhip/Lemon Squeezy. Publish the sales page |
| 25–30 | Launch at the early price. Ask first buyers for feedback and testimonials |

## 8. Success rules

1. Only list suppliers that passed vetting **and** agreed to be listed.
2. Never list replica/counterfeit sellers, no matter how much people ask.
3. Be specific about what you verified and when. Never write "guaranteed" or "100% legit".
4. Update the list. A supplier that stopped answering is a refund request waiting to happen.
5. Answer buyers fast. Your reputation *is* the product.
