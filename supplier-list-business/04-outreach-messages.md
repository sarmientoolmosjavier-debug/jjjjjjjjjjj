# Outreach Messages (English + 简体中文)

Send English first. Most Alibaba/Made-in-China sales reps read it. Add the Chinese version underneath for WeChat or 1688 sellers, or if they answer slowly. Replace anything in `[brackets]`.

Tips: keep messages short, use bullet points, one question per line. Polite but direct works best.

---

## #1. First inquiry (as a buyer)

**EN**
> Hello [name],
> I'm [your name] from [your brand/company]. I'm sourcing heavyweight hoodies for small streetwear brands.
> Could you tell me:
> 1. MOQ per design and per colour?
> 2. Available fabrics and GSM (e.g. 400 / 500 gsm French terry or fleece)?
> 3. Price per piece at 50 / 100 / 300 pcs with screen print or embroidery?
> 4. Sample cost and sample lead time?
> 5. Bulk production lead time?
> 6. Do you have your own factory? Could you send a short video of the production floor?
> Thank you!

**中文**
> 您好 [name]，
> 我是 [your brand/company] 的 [your name]，正在为小型街头潮牌采购重磅卫衣。
> 请问：
> 1. 每款、每个颜色的起订量是多少？
> 2. 有哪些面料和克重可选（例如 400 / 500 克毛圈或加绒）？
> 3. 50 / 100 / 300 件的单价分别是多少（含丝印或刺绣）？
> 4. 打样费用和打样周期？
> 5. 大货生产周期？
> 6. 请问是自己的工厂吗？可以发一段车间的短视频吗？
> 谢谢！

## #2. Ask for company registration details

**EN**
> Thank you for the information. Before placing a sample order, could you share your company's full Chinese legal name and Unified Social Credit Code? It's part of our standard supplier check. Thanks!

**中文**
> 感谢您的回复。下打样单之前，能否提供贵公司的中文全称和统一社会信用代码？这是我们供应商审核的常规步骤。谢谢！

---

## #3. Consent to be listed (the important one)

**EN**
> Hi [name], thank you, the sample quality was [good/excellent].
> I run a sourcing guide that recommends trusted factories to new clothing brands (mainly Europe and Latin America). I would like to include [company] in it, which would send you new small-brand customers.
> I would list: company name, location, products, MOQ, price range, and the business contact you choose.
> 1. Do you agree to be listed?
> 2. Which contact should I share (business email / WhatsApp / WeChat of the sales team)?
> 3. Is the MOQ and price range above correct?
> You can ask me to remove the listing at any time.

**中文**
> 您好 [name]，谢谢您，样品质量[很好/非常好]。
> 我在做一份采购指南，向新的服装品牌（主要是欧洲和拉丁美洲）推荐可靠的工厂。我想把 [company] 收录进去，可以为您带来新的小品牌客户。
> 收录内容包括：公司名称、地址、产品、起订量、价格区间，以及您指定的业务联系方式。
> 1. 您同意被收录吗？
> 2. 我应该公开哪个联系方式（业务邮箱 / 销售团队的 WhatsApp / 微信）？
> 3. 以上起订量和价格区间是否正确？
> 您可以随时要求我删除信息。

## #4. Referral commission / buyer discount

**EN**
> Would you offer something for customers who come from my guide? For example:
> - a [3–5]% commission to me on first orders, or
> - a discount or free sample for my readers with the code "[CODE]".
> This helps me keep your listing at the top. I will tell readers clearly that I may receive a commission.

**中文**
> 对于通过我的指南来的客户，您能提供一些优惠吗？例如：
> - 首单给我 [3–5]% 的佣金，或者
> - 给我的读者提供折扣或免费样品，使用代码"[CODE]"。
> 这样我可以把您放在推荐前列。我会明确告诉读者我可能会获得佣金。

## #5. Follow-up (no reply after 2–3 days)

**EN**
> Hi [name], just following up on my message about [hoodie samples / listing]. Is this something you can help with? Thank you!

**中文**
> 您好 [name]，跟进一下我之前关于[卫衣样品 / 收录]的消息，请问可以合作吗？谢谢！

---

## #6. Quarterly re-check

**EN**
> Hi [name], I'm updating my supplier guide. Are your MOQ ([X] pcs), price range ([X–Y]) and sample time ([X] days) still the same? Are you still taking small orders? Thanks!

**中文**
> 您好 [name]，我正在更新供应商指南。请问起订量（[X] 件）、价格区间（[X–Y]）和打样时间（[X] 天）还一样吗？现在还接小单吗？谢谢！

---

## #7. Polite no / removal

**EN**
> Thank you for your time. We won't be moving forward at the moment, but I'll keep your details for the future.

**中文**
> 感谢您的时间。我们目前暂不合作，但会保留您的信息以便将来联系。
