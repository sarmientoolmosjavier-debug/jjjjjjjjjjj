# Legal Basics & Policies

> Not legal advice. These are practical, commonly used templates. If you're in Spain/EU, a short consultation with a gestor or abogado about registering as autónomo and VAT on digital services is worth it once sales start.

## 1. Rules that keep you out of trouble

1. **No counterfeit/replica suppliers, ever.** Linking buyers to counterfeiters can expose you to trademark claims, and Payhip, Gumroad, Stripe and PayPal all ban it. They will freeze your balance.
2. **Consent before listing.** Only publish contacts that the supplier agreed to share (message #3). Keep the screenshots.
3. **Business contacts, not personal ones.** Company email, company WhatsApp Business, or the sales rep's *work* account. If a rep gives a personal number, ask for permission specifically and remove it on request.
4. **No scraping Alibaba.** Its terms forbid automated collection. Gather information by hand and from conversations.
5. **Honest claims.** Say what you checked and when. Avoid: "guaranteed", "100% legit", "never get scammed", income promises.
6. **Disclose commissions** wherever you receive them.
7. **Register and pay tax** once you're earning regularly. Using a merchant-of-record platform (Payhip, Lemon Squeezy) handles VAT collection on sales to EU consumers, but **you still declare your income**.

## 2. GDPR quick notes (if you or your buyers are in the EU)

- A **named person's** phone, WhatsApp or email is personal data, even if it's a work contact. A generic company email (sales@…) mostly isn't.
- Your legal basis for listing is **consent** (message #3) or legitimate interest for public business contacts. Consent is the clean option.
- Honour removal requests quickly (within 30 days at most; aim for 48h) and update the list for all buyers.
- Your own customer emails (from sales) also fall under GDPR, so have a privacy notice (section 5).

## 3. Disclaimer (put in the product + sales page footer)

> **Disclaimer.** This guide lists independent third-party suppliers. We are not affiliated with, and do not control, any supplier listed. "Verified" means that on the date shown we checked the company's registration, communicated with its sales team, and (where stated) inspected samples. It is not a guarantee of future product quality, pricing, delivery, or conduct. Any agreement you make is between you and the supplier. Always order samples and use protected payment methods. We are not responsible for losses arising from transactions with listed suppliers. Some suppliers may pay us a referral commission; this does not affect their score. All listed suppliers manufacture their own or unbranded/private-label products. We do not list sellers of counterfeit goods.

## 4. Terms of sale & refund policy (short version)

> **What you get:** instant access to the digital guide "[name]" and free updates for [12] months.
>
> **Personal use licence:** for your own sourcing. You may not resell, share publicly or republish the guide or its contents.
>
> **Refunds:** because this is instant-access digital content, all sales are final once you've accessed it, **except**: if more than [30]% of the listed suppliers don't respond to you within 14 days of purchase, email us with your messages/screenshots and we'll refund you in full or replace the suppliers.
>
> **Contact:** [your business email]. We reply within 48 hours.

*Why offer a conditional refund?* It removes the biggest buyer fear ("scam list"), and you control the risk by keeping the list fresh. It also reduces chargebacks, which can get your payment account suspended. EU note: to sell digital content without the 14-day withdrawal right, the buyer must **expressly consent and acknowledge** losing that right at checkout. Payhip and Lemon Squeezy offer a checkbox for this, so turn it on.

## 5. Privacy notice (for your site/checkout)

> We collect your name, email and payment details (processed by [Payhip/Lemon Squeezy]) only to deliver the product, send updates, and handle support. We don't sell your data. You can ask us to access or delete your data at [email]. Supplier information in the guide is published with each supplier's consent; suppliers can request removal at any time at the same email.
