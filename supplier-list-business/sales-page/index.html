Questions

Do these factories make replicas?

No. Every factory on the list makes unbranded or private-label products: you put your own brand on them. Sellers of copies are rejected.

I'm a total beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. We focused on low minimums (30–100 pieces) and factories that communicate clearly in English. The message templates and checklist walk you through the first order.

Do you earn commission from the factories?

Some factories may pay us a small referral fee or give our readers a discount. It costs you nothing and doesn't change how we score them.

How do I pay factories safely?

Use Alibaba Trade Assurance where available, or a protected method like a card or PayPal Goods & Services. Always order a sample first. The guide explains this step by step.
