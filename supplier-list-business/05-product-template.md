## Page 2. How to use this list (copy as-is, then edit)

1. Start with the **Top Picks**. They scored highest on our checks.
2. Message 2–3 suppliers, not just one, and compare quotes.
3. **Always order a sample before bulk production.**
4. Pay through a protected method (Alibaba Trade Assurance, or a card/PayPal Goods & Services) whenever possible.
5. Use the message templates on page [X].
6. Mention "[YOUR CODE]" for the reader discount where one is offered.

**What "verified" means here:** we checked that the company is registered and active in China, talked to its sales team, and (for Top Picks) ordered and inspected samples ourselves. It **does not** guarantee future quality, prices or delivery times. Factories change, so always order a sample.

**Disclosure:** some suppliers may pay us a small referral commission. It does not cost you anything and doesn't affect our scores.
