## Bundles you can sell

1. **Clothing Brand Starter Pack:** streetwear + hats + socks + labels/packaging. This is the strongest product
2. **Etsy / TikTok Shop Pack:** jewelry + candles + packaging
3. **Fitness Brand Pack:** activewear + drinkware + socks + packaging
4. **Pet Brand Pack:** pet accessories + packaging
5. **Summer Pack:** swimwear + eyewear + hats

Each bundle needs at least 8–10 **verified** suppliers before you sell it.

## Niche-specific red flags

- **Footwear / bags / watches:** listing photos with swooshes, monograms or brand-like logos ("inspired by", "AJ style", "Rolex style"). Alibaba guides with titles like "noob factory" exist. Reject anything like that.
- **Skincare:** "FDA approved" claims (FDA doesn't approve cosmetics), and no ingredient list or safety documents.
- **Drinkware:** no food-contact test report (LFGB for EU, FDA for US), or vague "304 steel" claims. Ask for the report.
- **Eyewear:** no UV400 test report or CE declaration.
- **Candles:** no IFRA certificate for fragrances.
- **Directory MOQs of "1+" or "2 pcs":** usually stock items or sampling, not custom production. Ask the real custom-production MOQ.

## Where these leads came from

- Global Sources, Made-in-China, Alibaba company profiles (supplier-written)
- SourceReady and Pietra Studio directories (aggregators; may route you through their platform)
- Supplier websites and "Top 10" blog posts (self-promotion)
- A few paid press releases (marked in notes)

None of these is independent proof. That's why the vetting step is the product.
